3Не ошибись! ✏️

  • i'm from kazakhstan. → I'm from Kazakhstan.
  • I speak english. → I speak English.
  • on monday → on Monday
  • Where are you from. → Where are you from?
  • films football and music → films, football and music
  • emma watson → Emma Watson
