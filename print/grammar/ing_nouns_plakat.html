1Глагол + ing = занятие 📖 стр. 90

+ ing
почти всегда
read → reading
draw → drawing
cook → cooking
e → ing
немую e убираем

dance → dancing
ride → riding
удвоить
короткое слово

2Где ставят 📖 стр. 90

I like reading, cooking and swimming. I'm very good at drawing. I'm going shopping. (без to!) Making a yurt is very difficult!

3Не ошибись! ✏️

  • good at draw → good at drawing
  • go to shopping → go shopping
  • swiming, shoping → swimming, shopping
  • makeing, danceing → making, dancing
  • Make a yurt is difficult. → Making a yurt…
