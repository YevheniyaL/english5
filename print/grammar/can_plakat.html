1Что значит can 📖 стр. 74

can

I can swim.
He can cook.

Penguins can't fly.

⚠️ can — одинаковый для всех ✏️
I can · he can (не cans) · they can
⚠️ после can — глагол как в словаре ✏️
he can swim — не swims, не to swim

2Вопрос и ответ 📖 стр. 74

can — вперёд: Can you dance?

умение → Yes, I can. / No, I can't.

просьба → Yes, of course. / No, sorry, I can't.

3Фразы для урока 📖 стр. 74

Sorry, can you say that again?

Can you spell that for me, please?

Can I open the window, please? ✏️

4Не ошибись! ✏️

  • He cans swim. → He can swim.
  • He can swims. → He can swim.
  • I can to swim. → I can swim.
  • Do you can swim? → Can you swim?
  • I don't can swim. → I can't swim.
  • can not → cannot / can't
