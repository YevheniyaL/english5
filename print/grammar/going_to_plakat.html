be going to

планы на будущее
English Plus 5 · Unit 8 (стр. 99, 101)

1Что это? 📖 стр. 99

be going to = «собираюсь» — планы.

Tomorrow we're going to go boating.

tomorrow · next week · this year · tonight ✏️

2Как строится 📖 стр. 99

am / is / are + going to + глагол

I'm going to play he / she's going to play you / we / they're going to play

3Утверждение, отрицание, вопрос 📖 стр. 99, 101

+
собираюсь
I'm going to win.
She's going to cook.
−
not перед going to
I'm not going to win.
He's not going to eat.
?
am / is / are — вперёд
Is he going to win?
— Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.
⚠️ Yes, I am. — не «Yes, I'm.» ✏️
What are you going to do? 📖
⚠️ going to + место = еду ✏️
I'm going to Baikonur. — еду в Байконур
I'm going to go to Astana. — собираюсь поехать

4Не ошибись! ✏️

  • I going to play. → I'm going to play.
  • She's going to plays. → She's going to play.
  • We're going play. → We're going to play.
  • I'm going to not play. → I'm not going to play.
  • Do you going to play? → Are you going to play?
  • Yes, I'm. → Yes, I am.
