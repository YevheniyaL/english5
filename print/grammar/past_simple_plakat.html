2be → was / were 📖 стр. 76

I / he / she / it — was

you / we / they — were

не было — wasn't / weren't

Was it cold? — Yes, it was.

3Одинаково для всех: I, he, they 📖 стр. 107, 109

+ ed
правильные
help → helped · want → wanted
love → loved (+ d)
study → studied · stop → stopped ✏️
своя форма
неправильные — учить
go → went · come → came · take → took
leave → left · tell → told · write → wrote

didn't
didn't + глагол как в словаре
I didn't find her.
He didn't go home.
Did…?
Did + кто + глагол?
Did you go home? — Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
What did you do yesterday?
⚠️ После did — глагол как в словаре ✏️
didn't go, не didn't went · Did you play?
⚠️ С be — без did ✏️
I wasn't there. · Were you at home?

4Не ошибись! ✏️

  • I goed. → I went.
  • She helpeds. → She helped.
  • I didn't went. → I didn't go.
  • Did you played? → Did you play?
  • Where you went? → Where did you go?
  • They was. → They were.
