1На чём едем? 📖 стр. 86

by
by + транспорт
by bus · by train · by car
by plane · by boat · by bike
on foot
пешком
He's travelling on foot.
⚠️ После by — ничего ✏️
не by a bus, не by my car → by bus, by car
⚠️ Пешком — только on ✏️
не by foot → on foot

2Вопрос 📖 стр. 86

How do you go to school?

— By bus. / On foot.

I am travelling by bus.

We're going by train.

3Расширение 📖 стр. 90

with — с кем-то: with my parents

for — для кого-то: a rug for her friend

without — без: without wool

4Не ошибись! ✏️

  • by the bus → by bus
  • by my car → by car
  • by foot → on foot
  • I go to school on bus. → by bus
  • a present with my mum → for my mum
  • without a wool → without wool
