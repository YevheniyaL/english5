2Кто? U1 📖 стр. 7

Astana is the capital city. It is big.

he — он · she — она · it — предмет, животное, город ✏️

you — ты и вы · I — всегда с большой буквы ✏️

4Кого? кому? U6 📖 стр. 71

После глагола и после with, for, to, at:

The students like her. · I play with him.

He likes she. → He likes her. ✏️

5Без предмета U7 расширение 📖 стр. 90

This is my bag. → The bag is mine.

Is this your dictionary? — No, it's not mine.

It's mine bag. → It's my bag. / It's mine. ✏️

6Не ошибись! ✏️

  • Me am ten. → I am ten.
  • Them are my friends. → They are
  • She likes he. → She likes him.
  • This is me bag. → my bag
  • Aliya and his dad → her dad
  • The bag is my. → mine
