1Когда? 📖 стр. 85

Действие происходит прямо сейчас:

Today we're cycling in the mountains.

Look! Harry is playing football. ✏️

Подсказки: now, at the moment, today, Look!, Listen! ✏️

2Формула 📖 стр. 85

am / is / are+глагол + ing
I am readingI'm reading
he / she / it is readingshe's reading
we / you / they are readingthey're reading

Harry is playing · Harry and Nic are playing

3Как пишется -ing? 📖 стр. 85

+ing
большинство глаголов
talk → talking
cook → cooking
play → playing
e + ing
на -e → e убираем
make → making
dance → dancing
write → writing
удвоить
одна гласная + одна согласная
swim → swimming
run → running
shop → shopping
sit → sitting
⚠️ Две гласные — не удваиваем ✏️
eat → eating · sleep → sleeping
⚠️ y не меняем, -ee не трогаем ✏️
study → studying · see → seeing

4Отрицание 📖 стр. 85

I'm not cooking.
Dad isn't cooking.
We aren't camping!

5Вопрос и ответ 📖 стр. 87

am / is / are — в начало:

Are you studying?Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
Is she walking?Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.

Where are you staying?

6Обычно или сейчас? 📖 стр. 85

every day, usually, often → Present Simple
I play football every Saturday.
now, at the moment, Look! → Present Continuous
Look! I am playing football.

7Не ошибись! ✏️

  • I playing → I am playing
  • He is play → He is playing
  • She is swiming → swimming
  • He is makeing → making
  • Is you reading? → Are you reading?
  • Yes, I'm. → Yes, I am.
