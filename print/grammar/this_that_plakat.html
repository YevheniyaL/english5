English Plus 5 · Unit 2 (стр. 20, расширение)

1Один или много? Рядом или далеко? 📖 стр. 20

this
один · рядом
This is a photo of me.
that
один · далеко
Is that your horse?
these
много · рядом
These are my cousins.
those
много · далеко
What are those tall buildings?
⚠️ this / that + is ✏️
This is my book. · That is my dog.
⚠️ these / those + -s + are ✏️
These are my books. · Those are my dogs.

2Не ошибись! ✏️

  • This are my sisters. → These are…
  • this books → these books
  • these book → these books
  • Those is my bike. → That is…
  • that people → those people
