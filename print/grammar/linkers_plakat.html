1and, but, or 📖 стр. 47

and
и — добавляем
He's tall and he's got dark hair.
but
но — наоборот
She's serious, but she's nice.
or
или — выбор
Is he older or younger?
⚠️ Перед but — запятая 📖
She's serious, but she's nice.
⚠️ В списке перед последним — and 📖
a cinema, a museum and a restaurant

2because — потому что 📖 стр. 61

because стоит перед причиной:

I like the carnival because the music is great.

Why? — Because … ✏️

3also — тоже, ещё 📖 стр. 33

с is / are — после: There is also a museum.

с другими глаголами — перед: I also like tennis. ✏️

в конце — too: I like it too. ✏️

4Расширение (Unit 6) 📖 стр. 76

when — когда · before — до · after — после

When he was a child, he loved poetry.

After school, I play football. ✏️

5Не ошибись! ✏️

  • serious but nice → serious, but nice
  • older and younger? → older or younger?
  • There also is… → There is also…
  • I like also tennis. → I also like tennis.
  • a cinema, a museum, a café → …, a museum and a café
