1Когда? ✏️

Привычки, то, что бывает регулярно:
I play football on Saturdays.

Факты: Cats like fish.

Подсказки: every day, on Mondays, always, usually, often, sometimes, never

2Утверждение 📖 стр. 57

I / you / we / theyhe / she / it
I play
They play		He plays
She plays

Проверка ✏️ — замени на местоимение:

Harry → he → plays Harry and Nic → they → play

3he / she / it: -s, -es или -ies? 📖 стр. 71

-s
большинство глаголов
speak → speaks
like → likes
eat → eats
-es
на -o, -sh, -ch, -x, -ss
go → goes · do → does
wash → washes · watch → watches
fix → fixes · kiss → kisses
-ies
согласная + y → -ies
study → studies
copy → copies
carry → carries
⚠️ Гласная + y → просто -s ✏️
play → plays · buy → buys · enjoy → enjoys
⚠️ Исключение ✏️
have → has  She has a cat.

4Отрицание 📖 стр. 59

He doesn't plays. → He doesn't play.

6always, often, never 📖 стр. 68

always всегда · usually обычно · often часто · sometimes иногда · never никогда

Стоят перед глаголом: He never watches TV.

✏️ но после am / is / are: She is always happy.

7Не ошибись! ✏️

  • He play → He plays
  • They plays → They play
  • He studys → He studies
  • She haves → She has
  • Does he plays? → Does he play?
  • They doesn't → They don't
