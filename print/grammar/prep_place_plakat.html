in / on / under / next to…

предлоги места
English Plus 5 · Unit 3 (стр. 31) · Unit 4 (стр. 47) · Unit 5 (стр. 62, расш.)

1Шесть предлогов урока 3.5 📖 стр. 31

in · on · under
в · на · под
in the bag · on the desk · under the table
next to · near · opposite
вплотную рядом · недалеко · напротив
next to the shoes · near the station
opposite the park
⚠️ Слово не меняется! ✏️
на столе, под столом → on the table, under the table
⚠️ на картинке, на фото → in ✏️
in the picture · in the photo

2Как строится фраза ✏️

что + is / are + предлог + the + место

The cat is under the table. The books are on the desk. There's a park near my house.

3Вопрос 📖 стр. 31–32

Where is the cat? — It's under the bed.

Is there a café near here? — Yes, there is.

next to = вплотную · near = недалеко ✏️

4На фото (Unit 4) 📖 стр. 47

on the left · on the right

in the middle

at the back · at the front

Sylvie is at the back on the left.

5Расширение (Unit 5) 📖 стр. 62

behind — за · in front of — перед

between — между (A and B)

above — над, не касаясь

6Не ошибись! ✏️

  • under table → under the table
  • The cat under the table. → The cat is under…
  • next the window → next to the window
  • opposite of the park → opposite the park
  • in front the house → in front of the house
  • on the picture → in the picture
  • in the left → on the left
